# Research Projects

A selection of my research work spanning biochar science, computational biology, and plant molecular biology.

**[PhD Research — Biochar & Soil Microbiology](phd-research.md)**

Synthesizing biochar from aquatic weeds via slow pyrolysis and studying its impact on crop growth, developmental gene expression, and soil microbial community dynamics. Includes DFT-based modelling of Extracellular Electron Transfer at the biochar–microbe interface.

`Biochar` `Slow Pyrolysis` `Soil Microbiology` `DFT` `Plant Biology`

**[MS Dissertation — Stress-Responsive Gene Promoters](ms-dissertation.md)**

Bioinformatic promoter analysis of four stress-responsive gene families (DREB1, SWEET, LIM, ANNEXIN) in *Thellungiella salsuginea* and *Arabidopsis thaliana* to understand osmotic stress tolerance mechanisms in Brassicaceae.

`Bioinformatics` `Promoter Analysis` `Brassicaceae` `Plant Stress` `Genomics`

**[Magnaporthe oryzae Effectors — Internship](magnaporthe-project.md)**

Secretome-wide in silico analysis of ~365 effector proteins from the rice-blast pathogen *Magnaporthe oryzae*, characterizing signal peptides, intrinsic disorder, phosphorylation sites, and post-translational modifications. Funded by SERB-DST, Government of India.
